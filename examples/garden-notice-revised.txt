Notice to all plot holders

The spring work day takes place on Saturday 13 April from 9:00 to 13:00.
Please bring gloves and a hand fork. The shed will be open, so wheelbarrows
and the larger tools can be borrowed on the day.

The water supply is switched on again from Monday 15 April. Until then,
please use the rain barrels behind the compost bays.

Plot fees for the year are due by the end of May. Pay at the kiosk or
leave an envelope in the letterbox by the gate.

Questions go to the committee mailbox in the shed. The next committee
meeting is on Tuesday 16 April at 19:00 in the shed.
