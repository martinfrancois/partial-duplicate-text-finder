Carrot and ginger soup

Serves four.

1 onion, chopped
2 tablespoons oil
600 g carrots, sliced
1 thumb of ginger, grated
1 litre vegetable stock
Salt and pepper

Soften the onion in the oil for five minutes. Add the carrots and ginger
and cook for another three minutes. Pour in the stock, bring to the boil
and simmer for twenty minutes until the carrots are soft. Blend until
smooth and season to taste.
